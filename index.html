Home Hub

Home Hub is a private home-automation connection used by a single household. It links that household's own Home Assistant server to the devices in their home, so the family can see who is at the front door and control the lights from their own assistant.

Home Hub is not a public service. It has no sign-up, and only members of the household it was built for can use it.

What it does with a Google account

When a household member signs in, Home Hub asks for access to the doorbell camera and smart-home devices on that same account. It uses that access only to show camera events and to carry out the commands that person gives, on the household's own server.
