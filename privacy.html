Home Hub privacy policy

Home Hub is a private home-automation connection run by one household for its own use. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What Home Hub accesses

With the permission of the household member who signs in, Home Hub accesses the doorbell camera and smart-home devices linked to that Google account: device status, camera events and clips, and the ability to send commands to those devices.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the household what is happening at their own home and to carry out commands they give. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than running the household's own home automation.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored on a server controlled by the household. Camera clips are deleted automatically after 90 days. Sign-in tokens are stored on the same server and are used only to keep the connection working.

Sharing

Home Hub does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone outside the household. Data is not transferred to third parties except where needed to provide the features above to the household itself.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on request by the account holder.

Contact

Questions go to the developer contact shown on the Google sign-in screen for Home Hub.
